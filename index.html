📜 Set Info
🏷 Brands (Origins)

A brand is where a character comes from. Characters from the same brand can Backup each other. Its shape shows up as placeholder art.

🤝 Allegiances (Tags & Partners)

Tags say what a card is. A host's Partners say which tags can attach to it as a Backup.

💾 Your save

Autosave is on. Everything lives in this browser on this device (…). To move to another device or hand your work to a collaborator, download a save file.

Opens save files (.lftset.json), export ZIPs, shared card PNGs, Excel (.xlsx), .csv or cards.json. You can also just drag files onto the page.

📊 Spreadsheet

Prefer typing cards in a spreadsheet? Grab the template, fill in rows, and drop the file back here. Stats and text update, and your art and styling stay.

🔗 Live Google Sheet

Keep the master list in Google Sheets and everyone's Forge pulls the latest version. In the sheet: Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer, then paste the link here.

📦 Export pack (ZIP)

📸 Snapshots & backups

Take a snapshot of your whole set, or of one deck (open it in Cards → 📸 Snapshot), before big changes. Roll back to any snapshot later, or download them all as one archive.

📱 Install

Add Card Forge to your home screen or desktop so it opens like an app and works offline.

On iPhone/iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen. On Chrome/Edge: the install icon in the address bar.
